How Inquiries Reach You

The contact form has no backend — submitting it opens the visitor's own email app with a pre-filled message addressed to dursigns@abcadmin9.online.
There is no database or inbox on the site itself — every inquiry lands in that email inbox, not here.
If you want submissions logged automatically instead of relying on email, that needs a form backend (e.g. a form service) or a real server.
